FESCO Unit Rate — November 2026

The full FESCO domestic electricity tariff for November 2026: every slab, every fixed charge and every surcharge, with plain explanations of protected status, lifeline, TOU and the slab-crossing effect.

The table shows only the energy rate. Your real per-unit cost is higher, because the fixed charge, FPA, quarterly adjustment, FC surcharge and 18% GST are added on top — an unprotected consumer at 350 units pays Rs 36.46 in energy charges but about Rs 49.74 per unit all-in.

Protected vs unprotected — the 6-month rule

Your category is printed on your bill, and it matters more than almost anything else. A protected consumer used 200 units or less in each of the last 6 consecutive months, and pays Rs 10.54 per unit for 1–100 units and Rs 13.01 for 101–200 units, with an FC surcharge of Rs 1.43 per unit.

An unprotected consumer is everyone else — anyone who went over 200 units in any one of the last 6 months. Unprotected rates start at Rs 22.44 per unit, more than double the protected rate, and the FC surcharge is Rs 3.23 per unit. Once protected status is lost, you need six straight months at or under 200 units to get it back.

Lifeline — the lowest tariff

Lifeline is the cheapest category of all, for very low usage: 1–50 units at Rs 3.95 per unit and 51–100 units at Rs 7.74 per unit, with no fixed charge and no FC surcharge. Above 100 units, you leave the lifeline category for that month.

TOU — time of use

TOU (time of use) charges by when you use electricity rather than how much. Peak hours are 6 PM to 10 PM, and peak electricity costs Rs 46.85 per unit. All other hours are off-peak, at Rs 34.53 per unit. The fixed charge is Rs 675 per kW. TOU suits consumers who can shift heavy use out of the 6–10 PM window.

The slab-crossing effect — one unit can cost over Rs 1,000

FESCO's domestic tariff is a single-slab tariff: unprotected consumers pay one rate on all units, not a different rate for each block. Crossing a slab boundary therefore raises the rate on your entire consumption, not just on the extra units. Here is a concrete example, using FPA +Rs 1.1086, QTA Rs 0 and a 1 kW sanctioned load:

Watch the 200-unit line twice over. Crossing 200 units moves you into a higher slab for that month — and can also cost you protected status for the next six months, roughly doubling your rate.

Fixed charge, FPA, QTA and GST — the other lines on your bill

Fixed charge is a monthly charge per kW of your sanctioned load — the load printed on your bill, usually 1–5 kW for a home. A 1 kW home at the 301–400 slab pays Rs 475 a month; a 3 kW home pays three times that. FPA (fuel price adjustment) is a per-unit charge that changes every month with fuel costs — for example, +Rs 1.1086 per unit for August 2026 consumption. QTA (quarterly tariff adjustment) is a per-unit adjustment that changes every quarter. FC surcharge (financing cost surcharge) is Rs 3.23 per unit for unprotected and TOU consumers and Rs 1.43 for protected consumers. Finally, GST at 18% is charged on the subtotal of all of the above.

Two charges that used to appear on bills are gone: the Rs 35 PTV fee and the 1.5% electricity duty were both removed in July 2025. If either still appears on a current bill, check it with FESCO — it should not be there.

How to lower your FESCO bill

  • Stay under a slab boundary. If you are near 100, 200, 300 or 400 units as the month ends, cut back — one unit over a boundary can add over Rs 1,000.
  • Stay protected. Keep every month at or under 200 units for six months running — that roughly halves your energy rate.
  • Check the meter reading. If the reading on your bill is higher than your physical meter, your bill is wrong. Compare the two and file a complaint if they do not match.

All rates on this page are from NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, effective 12 February 2026. FPA and QTA change regularly, so always confirm the figures on your own bill.

Frequently asked questions

What is the per-unit price for 300 units?

For an unprotected domestic consumer, 300 units is in the 201–300 slab, so the energy rate is Rs 33.10 per unit on all 300 units, plus a fixed charge of Rs 400 per kW, FPA, an FC surcharge of Rs 3.23 per unit and 18% GST.

Why did my rate change this month?

Usually a slab change — a few more units can push you into a higher slab and raise the rate on your whole bill — or losing protected status after one month over 200 units. The monthly FPA also changes, which moves the total even when the slab rate is the same.

What is the protected unit rate?

Rs 10.54 per unit for 1–100 units and Rs 13.01 for 101–200 units, with an FC surcharge of Rs 1.43 per unit. You are protected only if you used 200 units or less in each of the last 6 months.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The official PITC portal only accepts a 14-digit Reference Number or a 10-digit Customer ID. Use the bill checker on our homepage to validate your number, then check it on the official portal.
