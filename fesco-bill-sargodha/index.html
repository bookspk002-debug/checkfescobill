FESCO Bill — Sargodha (November 2026)

Sargodha is one of FESCO's eight districts and a major circle in the network, serving a city of urban households alongside the farming belt around it. The bill check is exactly the same as everywhere else in the FESCO region — one portal, one reference number — but what to watch for depends on whether you are in the city or out in the fields.

Updated: November 2026

How to check your FESCO bill in Sargodha

You need only one thing: the 14-digit reference number from any old bill, printed at the top-left and sometimes beginning with U (urban) or R (rural). There is no login, no fee and no separate Sargodha portal.

  1. Find your reference number. Use any previous FESCO bill, or the meter reading slip left by the meter reader. Save the number in your phone so you always have it.
  2. Open the official portal. Go to bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill and select FESCO. PITC runs billing for all distribution companies, so pick FESCO from the list.
  3. Enter the number and the captcha. Type your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), choose U or R if asked, and complete the image captcha.
  4. Read, print or download. The portal shows your latest bill with the amount, due date and units used, plus your last 12 months of billing history.

Weak mobile data? Use SMS. Text your reference number to 8118 and your bill details come back by SMS — no internet needed. That is often the easiest option in the villages around Sargodha city, where data coverage can be patchy. The toll-free helpline 118 is the other fallback.

Sargodha: city households and farm connections side by side

Sargodha is one of FESCO's eight districts, which together serve roughly 4 to 5.4 million consumers. FESCO's headquarters is in Faisalabad, but Sargodha is one of the major circles in the network, with a distinctive mix: urban households in the city, and a large surrounding agricultural belt where tube wells and farm connections sit alongside domestic ones.

That mix is what makes Sargodha bills worth reading carefully: a domestic connection in the city and an agricultural connection a few kilometres away are billed differently, and the difference is not just the units used. For rural and agricultural consumers, two lines on the bill deserve a close look every month: the tariff category and the sanctioned load.

The sanctioned load is the load your connection was approved for, printed on the bill, and the fixed charge is calculated per kW of that load — so a tube well sanctioned at a higher load carries a higher fixed charge every month, whether the pump ran or not. If the load or the tariff category on your bill does not match how the connection is actually used, raise it with FESCO rather than simply paying a bill that looks too high.

For city households, the more common surprise is the slab. Domestic consumers are "protected" — paying the lower protected rates — only if they used 200 units or less in each of the last six consecutive months. One month over 200 units loses protected status and roughly doubles the energy rate, so watch usage in the hot months.

November 2026 rates: the current domestic tariff is NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, effective 12 February 2026. Lifeline is Rs 3.95 (1–50 units) and Rs 7.74 (51–100); protected is Rs 10.54 (1–100) and Rs 13.01 (101–200); unprotected runs from Rs 22.44 (1–100) up to Rs 47.20 (700+ units). TOU peak (6–10 PM) is Rs 46.85 and off-peak Rs 34.53. On top come the FC surcharge (Rs 3.23 per unit; Rs 1.43 protected), monthly FPA, quarterly adjustment and 18% GST. The Rs 35 PTV fee and 1.5% electricity duty were removed in July 2025.

Practical tips for Sargodha consumers

  • Keep the reference number safe. Without it you cannot check the bill online at all — the portal does not accept CNIC, name, address or meter number. Save it in your phone and keep one old bill filed away.
  • Check before the due date. A late-payment surcharge is added once the due date passes, so check the bill as soon as it is issued — paper bills can arrive late in farming areas.
  • Use SMS where data is weak. If the portal's captcha will not load on a slow connection, texting your reference number to 8118 gets the same details.
  • Compare the meter reading. The bill shows the reading recorded by the meter reader. For tube wells and rural connections, compare it with the actual meter — if the reading is higher than the meter shows, file a complaint.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my FESCO bill in Sargodha?

Open the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill, enter your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), choose U or R if asked, and complete the image captcha. Your latest bill appears with the amount and due date. You can also text your reference number to 8118 or call helpline 118.

Does the same portal work for Sargodha?

Yes. The same official PITC portal covers every FESCO district, including Sargodha — there is no separate district portal. The same SMS service (8118) and helpline (118) apply everywhere.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No, only a 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID works. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The number is printed at the top-left of any old bill.
