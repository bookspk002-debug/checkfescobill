About FESCO Bill Online

FESCO Bill Online is an independent, free guide and set of tools for consumers of the Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO) in Pakistan. It explains your electricity bill in plain language and helps you check, estimate and pay it.

Updated: November 2026

What this website is

This website is an informational resource for FESCO consumers. It does not issue bills, collect payments or hold customer records. Everything here is free to use, with no accounts, no sign-up and no fees. There is no registered company behind this website; it is run as an independent informational project.

Your real bill is always the one issued by FESCO and shown on the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill. This website cannot show your real bill, change it or take a payment for it.

Why it was built

The official PITC portal works, but it gives no plain-language help. It shows a bill without explaining what each line means, why the amount changed, or what to do when something is wrong. Many other bill websites make the problem worse: they show outdated rates and copied text that does not match the current tariff.

This website was built to fill that gap. The tariff rates used here are verified against the current NEPRA notification, S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, and were last reviewed in November 2026. Each guide explains one thing clearly — how a charge is calculated, where to find a number on your bill, or what steps to take next — so you can check the answer against your own bill.

What the website offers

  • Bill checker — on the homepage, it validates the format of your 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID before you go to the official portal. The check runs on your own device; your number is never sent to our servers.
  • Bill calculator — the FESCO bill calculator estimates your bill from your units, consumer type and sanctioned load, with a full breakdown of energy charges, fixed charges, FPA, QTA, surcharge and GST.
  • Tariff tables — current unit rates for lifeline, protected, unprotected and TOU consumers, taken from NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026.
  • Guides — step-by-step help for paying a bill, downloading a duplicate bill, reading a bill line by line, checking a previous bill, applying for a new connection, using the SMS service, and filing a complaint.
  • City pages — local information for the main cities in the FESCO region, including Faisalabad, Jhang, Sargodha, Toba Tek Singh and Chiniot.

Sources

The information on this website is based on public sources: NEPRA tariff notifications, including S.R.O. 279(I)/2026; the official PITC bill portal; and the official FESCO website at fesco.com.pk. Where a figure changes often, such as the monthly fuel price adjustment (FPA), the page says which month the figure is from.

Not affiliated. This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO), the Power Information Technology Company (PITC) or NEPRA. For anything about your own bill — a wrong amount, a missing bill, a complaint or a new connection — contact FESCO directly on helpline 118 or through fesco.com.pk. This website cannot access your bill or fix it.

How often content is reviewed

Content is reviewed every month, and again whenever NEPRA notifies a new tariff. Even so, a rate can change between reviews, so always confirm the final amount on your official bill before paying. See our disclaimer for the full position.
