Privacy Policy

This website collects nothing from you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no newsletter and no forms that store your details. This page explains, in plain language, what happens — and what does not happen — when you use this website.

Updated: November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. We do not ask for your name, address, phone number, CNIC number or any other personal information, and we do not store anything you type into the tools on this website on our servers. There is no registered company behind this website and no customer database.

The bill checker

The bill checker on the homepage validates the format of your 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID — for example, whether it has the right number of digits. That check runs entirely in your own browser. Your reference number is never sent to our servers, and it is never stored by us.

When you click through to check your real bill, you leave this website and go to the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk. Anything you enter there is handled under that portal's own privacy policy, not this one.

Saving your reference number

The checker offers an optional "save my reference number" tick box. If you tick it, your reference number is stored only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is stored only if you tick the box. You can remove it at any time by unticking the box, clearing the saved number on the homepage, or clearing your browser's local storage. If you do not tick the box, nothing is saved.

The bill calculator

The bill calculator runs entirely in your browser. The units, consumer type and sanctioned load you enter are used only to calculate the estimate on your screen. None of those figures are sent to our servers or stored by us.

Cookies and tracking

No tracking cookies are installed by this site. We do not use analytics that follow you across websites, and we do not show advertising that tracks you. Your browser may still store the local-storage entry described above if you choose to save your reference number, but that stays on your device and is not a tracking cookie.

Third-party links

This website links to third-party sites, including the official PITC portal, the official FESCO website (fesco.com.pk) and the complaints portal (ccms.pitc.com.pk). Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we do not control what they collect. Please read their policies before entering your reference number, customer ID or personal details there.

Contact form

The contact form on the contact page is not connected to an inbox and sends nothing. Anything you type into it stays on the page and is not delivered to anyone. Do not enter your reference number, CNIC number or other personal details in that form. For problems with your own bill, contact FESCO directly on helpline 118.

Children's privacy

This website provides general information about electricity bills. It is not aimed at children, and because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a contact inbox is added in future — this page will be updated and the updated date at the top will change. The date currently shown is November 2026.
