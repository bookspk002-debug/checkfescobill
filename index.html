How to check your FESCO bill online

  1. Find your reference numberTake any old FESCO bill and look at the 14-digit Reference Number at the top-left, just below the FESCO logo. No old bill? Use the 10-digit Customer ID instead.
  2. Enter it in the checker aboveType the number and press Check Bill. The checker only validates the format on your own device — nothing is sent to us.
  3. Open your bill directlyClick “Open General Bill”. Your bill opens directly on the official PITC portal in a new tab — no need to type the number again, and no captcha.
  4. View, download or printYour latest bill appears with the amount due, the due date and your last 12 months of billing history. You can print it or save it as a PDF — that is your duplicate bill.

Note on U and R: some FESCO bills show a U (urban) or R (rural) letter after the reference number. Our checker accepts the number with or without the letter — the bill opens directly either way.

What you need — and what does not work

Bill checking in Pakistan works on two numbers only:

  • 14-digit Reference Number — printed on every bill, top-left. This is the fastest way.
  • 10-digit Customer ID — also printed on the bill; use it if the reference number is damaged or unreadable.

You cannot check a FESCO bill by CNIC number, by name, by address, or by meter number. The official system simply does not support it, and any site that promises a “CNIC bill check” is not using the official portal. If you have lost your bill completely, call the FESCO helpline 118 (toll-free) with your meter number and address — the subdivision office can look up your reference number.

Where FESCO supplies electricity

FESCO (Faisalabad Electric Supply Company) serves around 4 to 5.4 million consumers across 8 districts of Punjab: Faisalabad, Sargodha, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, Chiniot, Khushab, Mianwali and Bhakkar. Other ways to check your bill

By SMS: text your 14-digit reference number to 8118 and you will get your current bill amount and due date by reply. See the full steps on the FESCO bill SMS service page.

By phone: call the FESCO helpline 118 (toll-free) and give your reference number. The helpline can also register complaints — see how to file a FESCO complaint.

In person: any FESCO subdivision office or customer service centre can print your bill if you bring your reference number or CNIC.

Frequently asked questions

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The official PITC portal only accepts a 14-digit Reference Number or a 10-digit Customer ID.

Where is the reference number on a FESCO bill?

At the top-left of every FESCO bill, just below the FESCO logo. It is also printed on the payment counterfoil at the bottom. Some bills show a U (urban) or R (rural) letter next to it — the portal asks you to select U or R separately.

Is checking my FESCO bill online free?

Yes — the official PITC portal is free, with no login needed, and this website is free too. We never charge for the checker, the calculator or any guide.

How can I check my FESCO bill by SMS?

Text your 14-digit reference number to 8118. You will receive a reply with your current bill amount and due date. Standard SMS charges from your mobile network apply.

What if my FESCO bill is wrong or too high?

Compare the meter reading on the bill with your physical meter first. If they do not match, call 118 (toll-free) or lodge a complaint at ccms.pitc.com.pk. You can also visit your nearest FESCO subdivision office with the bill and your CNIC.

Can I download a duplicate FESCO bill?

Yes. Enter your reference number in the checker above and click “Open General Bill” — your latest bill opens directly on the official PITC portal with print and download options. Save it as a PDF or print it — it works as a duplicate bill for payment.
