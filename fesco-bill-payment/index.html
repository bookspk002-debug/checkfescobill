FESCO Bill Payment Online — November 2026

You do not need to queue at a bank to pay your FESCO bill. You can pay it from your phone in a few minutes with JazzCash or Easypaisa, through your bank's mobile or internet banking, at an ATM, or over the counter at a bank branch or FESCO customer service centre.

Updated: November 2026

What you need before you pay

Every payment method asks for the same two things: your 14-digit reference number and the amount due. The reference number is printed at the top-left of your bill and never changes. The amount due is the total payable on the current month's bill, including any arrears.

If you do not have the paper bill, download a duplicate bill from the official PITC portal first, or text your reference number to 8118 to confirm the latest amount. Do not guess the amount — paying less than the total due leaves arrears. If the amount looks wrong, check it with our bill calculator or read our guide on how to read your FESCO bill before you pay.

Verify before you confirm. Every app and ATM shows the consumer name and the amount on screen after you enter the reference number. Check that the name matches yours and the amount matches your bill. If the name is wrong, you have typed the wrong reference number — stop and check it again. A payment sent to the wrong reference number is very hard to get back.

Pay with JazzCash

JazzCash is one of the most common ways to pay a FESCO bill, because it works from any phone with the app and a JazzCash account balance.

  1. Open the JazzCash app and log in.
  2. Go to bill payments and choose electricity, then select FESCO from the list of companies.
  3. Enter your 14-digit reference number and tap to fetch the bill.
  4. Check the name and amount shown on screen against your bill.
  5. Confirm the payment and save the receipt and transaction ID shown afterwards.

Make sure your JazzCash balance covers the full bill amount before you start — if it is short, the payment will fail, so top up first.

Pay with Easypaisa

Easypaisa works in the same way as JazzCash, and the steps are almost identical.

  1. Open the Easypaisa app and log in.
  2. Go to bill payments and choose electricity, then select FESCO.
  3. Enter your 14-digit reference number and fetch the bill.
  4. Verify the consumer name and amount on screen.
  5. Confirm and keep the receipt — take a screenshot of the confirmation screen and note the transaction ID.

You can also pay at an Easypaisa shop or agent if you do not use the app. Take your bill or your reference number with you, and ask for a printed receipt before you leave.

Pay through your bank — mobile, internet banking, ATM or branch

Almost every bank in Pakistan offers FESCO bill payment through its mobile app or internet banking. Open your bank's app, go to bill payments, choose electricity and FESCO, enter your 14-digit reference number, verify the name and amount, and confirm. The payment is debited from your bank account straight away.

At an ATM, insert your card, choose bill payment, select electricity and FESCO, enter the reference number, check the amount shown, and confirm. Keep the printed ATM slip as your receipt. At a bank branch, take your bill — or a printed duplicate bill — to the counter; the cashier will stamp the payment counterfoil and give you a receipt. Keep both. You can also pay at a FESCO customer service centre, which is useful if you also need to ask about arrears or a billing problem in the same visit.

Pay by the due date

Every FESCO bill has a due date printed on it — pay on or before that date. After the due date, a late payment surcharge applies and will appear on your next bill. Paying online on the last day still counts as on time, as long as the payment goes through that day.

Do not leave payment to the last evening if you can avoid it — apps and bank systems can be slow or briefly unavailable, and a failed payment on the due date can still leave you late. Pay a day or two early when possible.

How long does payment take to reflect?

A payment made online or at a bank usually reflects on your FESCO account within one working day. That means it may not show immediately if you check the portal a few minutes after paying. Wait until the next working day, then check the portal — the bill should show as paid, and the next month's bill should not carry the amount as arrears.

Keep your receipt or transaction ID until the next bill arrives and confirms the payment. A screenshot of the confirmation screen, the SMS from your bank or wallet, or the stamped counterfoil all count as proof if the next bill still shows the amount as arrears.

Is there an extra fee for online payment? Most banks and mobile wallets do not charge an extra fee for a FESCO bill payment. Some apps or agents may apply a small service charge, and if they do, it is shown on the payment screen before you confirm. Check the total on that screen — if a charge appears that you did not expect, you can cancel before confirming and use a different method.

Frequently asked questions

Can I pay after the due date?

Yes. You can still pay after the due date through any of the methods on this page, but a late payment surcharge will apply and will appear on your next bill. Pay as soon as possible, and try to pay before the due date next month.

What if I paid but the next bill still shows arrears?

First, check that you paid the full amount due and that the reference number was correct. If both are right, contact your bank or payment app with the transaction ID and ask them to confirm the payment. If they confirm it went through, take the receipt to your FESCO subdivision office, or call the helpline on 118, and ask them to correct the arrears. You can also file a complaint with the receipt attached as proof.

Can I pay a FESCO bill without the reference number?

No. Every payment method needs the 14-digit reference number to identify your connection. If you have lost it, find it on any old bill, or recover it by calling helpline 118 or visiting your subdivision office with your CNIC and meter details.

Is there any extra fee for online payment?

Usually no. Most banks, JazzCash and Easypaisa do not charge an extra fee for FESCO bill payment. If a service charge applies, it is shown on the confirmation screen before you pay, so check the total before confirming.
