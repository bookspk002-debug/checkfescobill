Please read this page before using the tools and guides on this website. It explains what this website is, what it is not, and what you should check before paying a bill.

Updated: November 2026

No affiliation

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to Faisalabad Electric Supply Company (FESCO), the Power Information Technology Company (PITC) or NEPRA. It is an independent informational website. There is no registered company behind it. Any reference to FESCO, PITC or NEPRA on this website is only to explain where official information and services come from.

We do not issue bills

Bills are shown only on the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill. This website does not issue bills and cannot show your real bill. The bill checker on the homepage only validates the format of your 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID on your own device, so you can check that you have typed it correctly before going to the official portal.

Estimates only

The bill calculator gives estimates only and is not an official bill. It estimates your bill from the units, consumer type and sanctioned load you enter, using the notified tariff. Your official bill may differ because of your exact sanctioned load, the FPA and QTA of your billing month, arrears, adjustments or a late-payment surcharge. Always confirm the amount on your official bill before paying, and never pay an amount taken only from this website.

Your reference number

No reference numbers are stored on our servers. The bill checker validates your number in your own browser, and the optional "save my reference number" feature stores it only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device, and only if you tick the box. See our privacy policy for the full explanation.

Tariff rates change

Tariff rates on this website are taken from NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026 and were last reviewed in November 2026. But FPA, QTA and tariffs change — FPA changes every month and QTA changes every quarter, and NEPRA can notify a new tariff at any time. A rate shown here may already be out of date by the time you read it. Always verify the rate and the final amount on official sources, such as your official bill, the PITC portal or fesco.com.pk, before making a payment or a decision.
