FESCO Bill SMS Service — November 2026

No internet, no problem. Text your 14-digit reference number to 8118 and FESCO replies with your current bill amount and due date — a quick check that works from any basic mobile phone.

Updated: November 2026

How the FESCO SMS service works

FESCO, the Faisalabad Electric Supply Company, serves around 4 to 5.4 million consumers across eight districts. Not every consumer has a smartphone or reliable internet, so FESCO runs a simple SMS service alongside its online portal: you send one text message, and you get your bill details back by text.

The service works like this: you text your 14-digit reference number to the short code 8118. The system looks up that reference number and sends a reply message showing your current bill amount and the due date. That is all there is to it — no app to install, no account to create, no login and no password.

How to check your bill by SMS — step by step

  1. Find your 14-digit reference number. It is printed at the top-left of any old FESCO bill, and it does not change month to month. Some reference numbers carry a U (urban) or R (rural) marker; for the SMS, send the 14 digits as printed.
  2. Open your phone's messaging app. Start a new message to the number 8118.
  3. Type only the 14-digit reference number. No extra words, no name, no CNIC number. The system reads the digits only, and extra text can stop it from understanding your message.
  4. Send the message and wait for the reply. The reply shows your current bill amount and the due date. Keep it on your phone until you have paid.

Exact format to send: a message containing only your 14-digit reference number, sent to 8118. For example, if your reference number is 14123456789012, your message is exactly that — nothing before it, nothing after it.

What the SMS reply shows — and what it does not

The reply gives you the two things most people need most urgently: how much you owe and when it is due. That is enough to pay on time and avoid a late-payment surcharge.

What it does not give you is the full bill. The SMS shows the latest bill only — no 12-month history, meter reading, units consumed, or breakdown of charges such as energy charges, fixed charges, FPA and GST. For those, use the official online portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill, which shows the latest bill plus 12 months of history with print and download options.

Is the SMS service free?

The service itself is free — FESCO does not charge you for the bill information. However, your mobile network's standard SMS charge applies to the message you send to 8118, just as it would for any other text message. The reply you receive does not cost you anything extra.

What to do if you get no reply

If you send the message and nothing comes back, work through these checks in order:

  • Count the digits. The reference number must be exactly 14 digits. The most common mistake is sending 13 digits by dropping one, or sending the 10-digit Customer ID instead.
  • Check for extra text. Make sure the message contains only the number. Words like "bill", your name or your CNIC can confuse the system.
  • Check your signal. If you are in an area with weak coverage, the message may not have gone out. Move to a spot with better signal and try again.
  • Wait a few minutes and try again. Short codes can be slow at busy times, such as just after bills are issued.
  • Use the online portal instead. If the SMS still does not work, check your bill on the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill, or call the FESCO helpline on 118 (toll-free).

Keep your mobile number updated with FESCO

The SMS service works in both directions. As well as checking your bill by text, FESCO can send bill SMS alerts to your registered mobile number — a message when your bill is issued, and sometimes a reminder before the due date. These alerts only reach you if the number FESCO has on file is your current one.

If you have changed your number, or never gave FESCO one, visit your nearest FESCO subdivision office and ask for your mobile number to be updated against your reference number. Take your CNIC and a copy of a recent bill. Keeping the number current is the simplest way to make sure you know when your bill is ready and when it is due — especially if the paper bill is delayed or never arrives.

Do not share your reference number carelessly. Anyone who has it can look up your bill amount. Only send it to 8118 or enter it on the official PITC portal. Never pay anyone who texts you claiming to be FESCO and asking for money — FESCO does not collect payments by SMS. This site never asks for your reference number and does not store any on its servers.

SMS vs the online portal — which should you use?

Use SMS when you just need the amount and the due date quickly, or when your internet is down. Use the online portal when you need the full bill — to print a duplicate bill, to check your 12-month history, or to confirm the charges before filing a complaint about a wrong bill. Both use the same 14-digit reference number. Remember: neither can find your bill by CNIC, name, address or meter number. If you have lost your reference number entirely, find it on an old bill or ask at your subdivision office — it cannot be recovered by texting your CNIC to 8118.

Frequently asked questions

What number do I text to check my FESCO bill?

Text your 14-digit reference number to 8118. Send only the number, with no extra words.

What does the FESCO SMS reply show?

The reply shows your current bill amount and its due date. It does not show your bill history, meter reading or charge breakdown — for those, use the official online portal.

Is the FESCO SMS service free?

The service itself is free, but your mobile network's standard SMS charge applies to the message you send to 8118.

What if I get no reply to my SMS?

Check that you sent exactly 14 digits with no extra words, check your network signal, and try again. If it still does not work, use the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill or call the FESCO helpline on 118.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The SMS service works only with a 14-digit Reference Number.
