FESCO Bill — Toba Tek Singh (November 2026)

Toba Tek Singh is one of FESCO's eight districts, a farming district of small towns — Toba Tek Singh itself, Gojra and Kamalia — surrounded by villages. Most consumers live outside the towns, so the U/R marker matters, mobile data is often weak, and the paper bill is not always on time. The check is the same everywhere in the FESCO region, and a text message works on any phone.

How to check your FESCO bill in Toba Tek Singh

All you need is the 14-digit reference number from any old bill — the only key the portal accepts, not your CNIC, name or meter number.

  1. Find your reference number — and its U or R. It is at the top-left of any previous bill: 14 digits, marked U (urban) or R (rural). Most village connections are R; town connections are usually U.
  2. Use our homepage checker. Enter your number in the checker at the top of our homepage — it is the fastest way to reach the official portal.
  3. Click Open General Bill. Your bill opens directly on the official PITC portal in a new tab — no need to type the number again, and no captcha.
  4. Read, print or download. The portal shows your latest bill with the amount, due date and units used, plus your last 12 months of billing history.

Toba Tek Singh: a farming district of villages and small towns

Toba Tek Singh sits inside FESCO's eight-district region — Faisalabad, Sargodha, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, Chiniot, Khushab, Mianwali and Bhakkar — which together serve roughly 4 to 5.4 million consumers. FESCO's headquarters is in Faisalabad, and Toba Tek Singh is served by the same network, the same portal, the same helpline 118 and the same subdivision offices as the rest of the region. What is different here is the consumer base: alongside the three towns, a large share of consumers live in villages, and many connections serve farms and tube wells as well as homes.

For village households, the most common billing problem is not the tariff — it is not seeing the bill in time. Paper bills can arrive late, and a bill you never saw still carries a late-payment surcharge after its due date. Checking online or by SMS removes that risk: both show the amount and due date as soon as the bill is issued. Also compare the reading on the bill with your meter — if it is higher than the meter shows, file a complaint.

For agricultural consumers, watch the tariff category and the sanctioned load printed on the bill. The fixed charge is calculated per kW of sanctioned load, so a tube well sanctioned at a higher load costs more every month even if the pump barely ran. If the category or load does not match how the connection is actually used, raise it with FESCO.

SMS is the easiest option in Toba Tek Singh. Text your reference number to 8118 and your bill details come back by SMS — no internet and no browser needed. Helpline 118 is the backup.

November 2026 rates — what Toba Tek Singh consumers pay

The current domestic tariff is NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, effective 12 February 2026: lifeline Rs 3.95 (1–50) and Rs 7.74 (51–100); protected Rs 10.54 (1–100) and Rs 13.01 (101–200); unprotected Rs 22.44 (1–100) to Rs 47.20 (700+). On top come the FC surcharge (Rs 3.23/unit; Rs 1.43 protected), monthly FPA, quarterly adjustment and 18% GST. The Rs 35 PTV fee and 1.5% duty were removed in July 2025.

One rule deserves attention in a rural district: domestic consumers keep the lower protected rates only if they used 200 units or less in each of the last six consecutive months. A single month over 200 units ends protected status and roughly doubles the energy rate. To estimate a domestic bill, use our FESCO bill calculator with your units and sanctioned load; agricultural connections are billed on their own tariff.

Practical tips for Toba Tek Singh consumers

  • Save the reference number — with its U or R. Keep the full number, letter included, in your phone — without it you cannot check the bill online.
  • Use SMS if your internet connection is slow. Texting the reference number to 8118 gives the same details.
  • Check the bill even if the paper copy has not arrived. Rural delivery can be late, but the due date does not wait — check by SMS each month to avoid the surcharge.
  • Compare the meter reading. The bill shows the reading recorded by the meter reader. For tube wells and rural connections, compare it with the actual meter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my FESCO bill in Toba Tek Singh?

Enter your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), including the U or R shown on your bill, in the checker on our homepage and click Open General Bill — your bill opens directly on the official PITC portal, with no re-typing and no captcha. Your latest bill appears with the amount and due date. You can also text your reference number to 8118 or call helpline 118.

What does U/R mean on my bill?

U stands for urban and R stands for rural. The letter is part of your reference number and tells the portal whether your connection is in a town or a village. Choose the same U or R on the portal's selector as printed on your bill — the wrong one can stop the bill from loading. Most village connections in Toba Tek Singh are R; town connections are usually U.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No, only a 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID works. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The number is printed at the top-left of any old bill.
