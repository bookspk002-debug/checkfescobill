FESCO Bill — Faisalabad (November 2026)

Faisalabad is the home of FESCO itself — the company's headquarters city and its largest consumer base. Whether you live in a dense urban neighbourhood or run a textile workshop, your bill is checked the same way: with the 14-digit reference number from any old bill.

Updated: November 2026

How to check your FESCO bill in Faisalabad

The check takes about a minute, costs nothing, and needs no login.

  1. Find your reference number. It is the 14-digit number at the top-left of any old FESCO bill, sometimes beginning with U (urban) or R (rural) — the portal has a U/R selector. It is also on the meter reading slip.
  2. Open the official portal. Go to bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill and select FESCO. PITC runs billing for all distribution companies, so make sure you pick FESCO, not another company.
  3. Enter the number and the captcha. Type your 14-digit reference number (or your 10-digit Customer ID instead) and complete the image captcha.
  4. Read your bill. The portal shows your latest bill — amount payable, due date and units used — plus your last 12 months of billing history. You can print it or download a copy.

Keep the number saved in your phone so you never have to hunt for a paper bill again.

No internet? Use SMS. Text your reference number to 8118 and you will get your bill details back by SMS. This works on any mobile phone and is handy when your data connection is slow. The toll-free helpline 118 can also help with bill queries.

Faisalabad: FESCO's headquarters city

Faisalabad is one of the eight districts FESCO serves — the others are Sargodha, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, Chiniot, Khushab, Mianwali and Bhakkar — and it is by far the most important. FESCO's headquarters is here, and the city holds the company's largest concentration of consumers, within a total consumer base of about 4 to 5.4 million across the whole FESCO region.

That matters because Faisalabad is not a purely residential city. It is a major textile-industry city, so FESCO serves a wide mix here: dense urban households, small power looms and textile workshops, dyeing and processing units, and the shops that support them. A domestic consumer and a workshop owner on the same street can have very different bills, even for similar units, because their tariff category and sanctioned load are different.

For households, the same reference-number check works for every Faisalabad consumer, domestic or commercial. For textile and workshop consumers, check the tariff category and sanctioned load printed on the bill: the fixed charge depends on your sanctioned load, so a connection registered with a higher load costs more every month even before a single unit is used.

Ways to check and pay in Faisalabad

Most Faisalabad consumers will never need to visit an office. The portal, SMS 8118 and helpline 118 cover checking, and the bill can be paid through the usual channels — see our FESCO bill payment guide. If you do need to visit in person, subdivision offices in the city serve different areas; call 118 to be directed to the right one. We do not publish street addresses here because they change.

November 2026 rates: the current domestic tariff is NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, effective 12 February 2026. Lifeline is Rs 3.95 (1–50 units) and Rs 7.74 (51–100); protected is Rs 10.54 (1–100) and Rs 13.01 (101–200); unprotected runs from Rs 22.44 (1–100) up to Rs 47.20 (700+ units). TOU peak (6–10 PM) is Rs 46.85 and off-peak Rs 34.53. Added on top are the FC surcharge of Rs 3.23 per unit (Rs 1.43 protected), monthly FPA, quarterly adjustment and 18% GST. The Rs 35 PTV fee and 1.5% electricity duty were removed from bills in July 2025.

Practical tips for Faisalabad consumers

  • Check before the due date. Bills carry a late-payment surcharge once the due date passes, so check your bill as soon as it is issued.
  • Estimate first, then verify. Use our FESCO bill calculator to estimate your bill from your units, then compare it with the official figure. A big gap usually means a different sanctioned load, arrears, or a slab crossing.
  • Watch the slab boundary. Unprotected consumers pay one slab rate on all units, so crossing from 200 to 201 units raises the rate on the whole bill.
  • Textile and workshop consumers: check your tariff category. Industrial and commercial connections are billed on different tariffs from domestic ones. If your bill's category does not match how you actually use the connection, raise it with FESCO.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my FESCO bill in Faisalabad?

Open the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill, enter your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), choose U or R if asked, and complete the image captcha. Your latest bill appears with the amount and due date. You can also text your reference number to 8118 or call helpline 118.

Where is the FESCO office in Faisalabad?

FESCO's headquarters is in Faisalabad, and subdivision offices across the city serve different areas. We do not publish a street address here because office locations change — call the toll-free helpline 118 with your area or reference number to find the office that serves your connection.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No, only a 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID works. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. If you have lost your bill, the number is also on the meter reading slip.
