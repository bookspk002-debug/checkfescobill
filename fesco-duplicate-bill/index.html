FESCO Duplicate Bill Download — November 2026

Lost your paper bill, or it never arrived? You can download a duplicate FESCO bill online in a few minutes and print it at home. It is the same bill, with the same amount and due date — and it is valid for payment everywhere the original is accepted.

Updated: November 2026

What is a FESCO duplicate bill?

A duplicate bill is simply a fresh copy of your current electricity bill, printed from the official billing system. It is not a different bill and does not change anything you owe: it shows the same 14-digit reference number, units, amount payable and due date as the paper bill delivered to your home.

Yes, a duplicate bill is valid for payment. Banks, ATMs, JazzCash, Easypaisa and FESCO customer service centres all accept it, because payment is made against the reference number printed on it. Make sure the payment counterfoil at the bottom prints clearly — it is the part the bank keeps.

This website is not affiliated with FESCO or PITC. The duplicate bill itself always comes from the official PITC portal, which runs billing for all electricity distribution companies in Pakistan. We never ask for your reference number and we do not store reference numbers on our servers.

How to download your FESCO duplicate bill

You need only one thing before you start: your 14-digit reference number, found at the top-left of any old FESCO bill. If you have a 10-digit customer ID instead, that works too. There is no login and no fee for viewing or printing a bill.

  1. Open the bill checker on the homepage. Go to the bill checker and type your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit customer ID).
  2. Click “Open General Bill”. Your bill opens directly on the official PITC portal — no need to type the number again, and no captcha.
  3. View your bill. Your latest bill appears on screen, showing the units consumed, the amount due, the due date and any arrears. Check that the consumer name and address match yours before you print.
  4. Print or save as PDF. Use your browser's print option (usually Ctrl + P on a computer) to print the bill, or choose "Save as PDF" as the printer to keep a digital copy on your phone or computer.

The whole process takes only a few minutes when the portal is working normally. If the page loads slowly, try again after a short while — the portal is busiest in the days just after bills are issued each month. Before you pay, check the amount against your own meter reading: if the units look far higher than usual, read our guide on how to read your FESCO bill and compare the readings.

How to get a duplicate bill without the bill in hand

Most people who need a duplicate bill do not have the original bill — that is the whole problem. The reference number is the key, so the first step is always to recover it.

The easiest way is to find any old FESCO bill. The reference number does not change from month to month, so a bill from last month or last year has the same number. Once you have it, write it down somewhere safe — you will need it every month.

If the number itself is lost, call the FESCO helpline on 118 (toll-free) or visit your local subdivision office with your CNIC and meter details. The office can look up your connection and give you the reference number. Note that the CNIC is used to identify you at the office — it cannot be typed into the online portal to check a bill. SMS 8118 can confirm your latest bill amount, but it cannot print a duplicate bill or look up your account without the reference number.

Printing tips

Print the full page, not just the top half — the payment counterfoil at the bottom is the part the cashier stamps and keeps. If your printer cuts off the edges, set the page scale to "Fit to page", or save the bill as a PDF first and print from the PDF.

Check that the reference number, the amount and the due date are clear and readable before you leave for the bank — a faded or blurry print can be refused. If you pay online through JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank mobile banking, you may not need a printed copy at all, since those apps only ask for the reference number and the amount. Still, save the PDF as a record. See our FESCO bill payment guide for the steps for each method.

What to do if the portal shows an old bill

Sometimes the portal shows last month's bill instead of the current one. This usually means the new bill has not been uploaded yet, or the billing cycle for your area has not finished. Check the bill month and due date on screen, and wait a day or two and check again.

If the portal keeps showing an old bill after the new billing month has started, double-check the reference number — a single wrong digit will show someone else's bill or no bill. If the number is correct and the problem continues, call the FESCO helpline on 118 or visit your subdivision office. Do not pay an old bill amount thinking it is the current one: the amount changes every month, and paying the wrong amount can leave arrears. Check your previous bills and 12-month history to see which months are shown.

Check before you pay. Always confirm the bill month, the amount and the due date on the duplicate bill before paying. A duplicate bill is only useful if it is the current month's bill.

Frequently asked questions

Is a duplicate bill valid for payment?

Yes. A duplicate bill from the official PITC portal shows the same reference number, amount and due date as the original, and it is accepted for payment at banks, ATMs, JazzCash, Easypaisa and FESCO customer service centres.

Can I get a duplicate bill by SMS?

No — not a printable copy. Texting your reference number to 8118 gives you the latest bill details by reply, but to print a duplicate bill you need to use the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill.

Can I check a duplicate bill by CNIC?

No. A FESCO bill cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The portal only accepts a 14-digit reference number or a 10-digit customer ID.

What if I lost my reference number?

Look for any old FESCO bill — the reference number is printed at the top-left and never changes. If you have no old bill, call the FESCO helpline on 118 or visit your subdivision office with your CNIC and meter details.
