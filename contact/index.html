Contact Us

There are two different ways to get help, depending on what your question is about. Please read both below so your message reaches the right place.

Updated: November 2026

If it is about your own bill — contact FESCO directly

For anything about your bill, contact FESCO itself. That includes a wrong amount, a bill that never arrived, a complaint, a new connection, a meter problem or load shedding. This website cannot access your bill, change it or fix it, and it cannot check the status of a complaint for you.

  • FESCO helpline: 118 (toll-free) — for bill queries, complaints and general help.
  • Official FESCO website: fesco.com.pk
  • Complaints portal: ccms.pitc.com.pk
  • Official bill portal: bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill — check your bill with your 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID.

When you call 118, keep your 14-digit Reference Number from your bill nearby — FESCO will usually ask for it. Our complaint guide explains what information to have ready before you call or file a complaint online.

Please do not send bill problems through the form below. The form is not connected to an inbox and cannot reach FESCO. Bill problems should go to FESCO on 118.

If it is about this website — use the form below

Use the form below only for feedback or corrections to this website — for example, a rate that looks out of date, a broken link, a mistake on a page, or a suggestion for a guide that would help. Do not include your full reference number, CNIC number or other personal details in your message.

Frequently asked questions

Can this website fix a problem with my FESCO bill?

No. This website cannot access your bill, change it or fix it. For a wrong amount, a missing bill, a complaint, a new connection or load shedding, contact FESCO on helpline 118 (toll-free) or through fesco.com.pk.

What is the FESCO helpline number?

The FESCO helpline is 118 (toll-free). You can also use the official FESCO website at fesco.com.pk and the complaints portal at ccms.pitc.com.pk.

Does the contact form on this page send my message?

No. The form on this page is not connected to an inbox yet, so it does not send or deliver your message anywhere. Do not send bill problems through it — contact FESCO on 118 instead.
