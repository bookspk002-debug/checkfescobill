FESCO Bill — Khushab (November 2026)

Khushab is one of FESCO's eight districts, a spread-out district where town households sit alongside villages, farms and the Salt Range foothills. Distances are long and many consumers live far from the town, so the paper bill can be late and mobile data can be weak — the check itself is the same everywhere in the FESCO region, and a text message works on any phone.

Updated: November 2026

How to check your FESCO bill in Khushab

All you need is the 14-digit reference number from any old bill — the only key the portal accepts, not your CNIC, name or meter number.

  1. Find your reference number — and its U or R. It is at the top-left of any previous bill: 14 digits, marked U (urban) or R (rural). Most village connections are R; town connections are usually U.
  2. Open the official portal. Go to bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill and select FESCO from the list.
  3. Enter the number, pick U or R, and solve the captcha. Type your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), match the portal's U/R selector to your bill, and complete the image captcha.
  4. Read, print or download. The portal shows your latest bill with the amount, due date and units used, plus your last 12 months of billing history.

Khushab: a spread-out district, far from the town centre

Khushab sits inside FESCO's eight-district region — Faisalabad, Sargodha, Jhang, Toba Tek Singh, Chiniot, Khushab, Mianwali and Bhakkar — which together serve roughly 4 to 5.4 million consumers. FESCO's headquarters is in Faisalabad, and Khushab is served by the same network, the same portal, the same helpline 118 and the same subdivision offices as the rest of the region. What is different here is distance: a large share of consumers live in villages and farming settlements well outside the town.

For village households, the most common billing problem is not the tariff — it is not seeing the bill in time. Paper bills can arrive late over long rural routes, and a bill you never saw still carries a late-payment surcharge after its due date. Checking online or by SMS removes that risk: both show the amount and due date as soon as the bill is issued. Also compare the reading on the bill with your meter — if it is higher than the meter shows, file a complaint.

For agricultural consumers, watch the tariff category and the sanctioned load printed on the bill. The fixed charge is calculated per kW of sanctioned load, so a tube well sanctioned at a higher load costs more every month even if the pump barely ran. If the category or load does not match how the connection is actually used, raise it with FESCO.

SMS is the easiest option in Khushab. Text your reference number to 8118 and your bill details come back by SMS — no internet, no captcha, no browser. Helpline 118 is the backup.

November 2026 rates — what Khushab consumers pay

The current domestic tariff is NEPRA S.R.O. 279(I)/2026, effective 12 February 2026: lifeline Rs 3.95 (1–50) and Rs 7.74 (51–100); protected Rs 10.54 (1–100) and Rs 13.01 (101–200); unprotected Rs 22.44 (1–100) to Rs 47.20 (700+). On top come the FC surcharge (Rs 3.23/unit; Rs 1.43 protected), monthly FPA, quarterly adjustment and 18% GST. The Rs 35 PTV fee and 1.5% duty were removed in July 2025.

One rule deserves attention in a rural district: domestic consumers keep the lower protected rates only if they used 200 units or less in each of the last six consecutive months. A single month over 200 units ends protected status and roughly doubles the energy rate. To estimate a domestic bill, use our FESCO bill calculator with your units and sanctioned load; agricultural connections are billed on their own tariff.

Practical tips for Khushab consumers

  • Save the reference number — with its U or R. Keep the full number, letter included, in your phone — without it you cannot check the bill online.
  • Use SMS if the portal captcha is hard on a slow connection. Texting the reference number to 8118 gives the same details.
  • Check the bill even if the paper copy has not arrived. Rural delivery can be late, but the due date does not wait — check by SMS each month to avoid the surcharge.
  • Compare the meter reading. The bill shows the reading recorded by the meter reader. For tube wells and rural connections, compare it with the actual meter.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check my FESCO bill in Khushab?

On the official PITC portal (bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill), enter your 14-digit reference number (or 10-digit Customer ID), choose U or R as printed on your bill, and complete the captcha. Your latest bill appears with the amount and due date. You can also text your reference number to 8118 or call helpline 118.

My paper bill arrives late — what should I do?

Check the bill online or by SMS as soon as it is issued each month, instead of waiting for the paper copy. Text your 14-digit reference number to 8118, or open the official PITC portal at bill.pitc.com.pk/fescobill — both show the amount and due date immediately. The due date does not move just because the paper bill was late.

Can I check FESCO bill by CNIC?

No, only a 14-digit Reference Number or 10-digit Customer ID works. FESCO bills cannot be checked by CNIC number, name, address or meter number. The number is printed at the top-left of any old bill.
